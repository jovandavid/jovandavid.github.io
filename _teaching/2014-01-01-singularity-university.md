2014 – 2017. About 10 courses per year throughout the GSP term, for audiences from AI experts to complete newcomers, with 10 to 25 participants per course. Subjects: introduction to AI, neural networks, deep learning, basic robotics, applied robotics, and the IBM Watson workshop (2015, 2016, 2017). Directly supported more than 50 startups and technology projects in building AI- and robotics-based solutions.
