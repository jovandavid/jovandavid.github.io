Staff Scientist, [Neural Computation Unit (Prof. Kenji Doya)](https://groups.oist.jp/ncu), [Okinawa Institute of Science and Technology (OIST)](https://www.oist.jp/), Okinawa, Japan — computational neuroscience and neurodegeneration modeling as part of the [Brain/MINDS 2.0](https://brainminds.jp/) consortium.

Director, [Exponential Japan KK](https://www.exponential.jp/) — AI/DX strategy, training and advisory for organizations including Sony HQ, the United Nations (OICT) and the WTO.

Representative Director, SURAIERA Inc. — drone × AI startup incubated at the OIST Innovation incubator (wildfire prediction, satellite AI).

Contact: jovandavid2(at)gmail(dot)com · [jovandavid.com](https://jovandavid.com)

I have been working in AI since 1997, when I was an intern in the first master's program in artificial intelligence in Latin America. My work moves between three things: research (computational neuroscience, spiking/neuromorphic computing, multi-agent LLM systems, LLM value alignment across languages), implementation (production pipelines on HPC/Slurm, a granted US patent), and dialogue with decision-makers (40+ keynotes across the US, Japan and Mexico). Previously: Chair AI Researcher at FAIRE, The University of Tokyo (2017–2022); AI & Robotics Fellow, Singularity University at NASA Research Park (2014–2017), where I was also a member of the inaugural 2009 GSP class; Postdoctoral Associate, University of Louisville (2010–2013). Ph.D. (Dr. Eng.) in Computer Science & Engineering, Kanazawa University, 2009.
