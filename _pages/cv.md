Researcher and educator working in artificial intelligence since 1997. Staff Scientist at the Neural Computation Unit (Prof. Kenji Doya), Okinawa Institute of Science and Technology (OIST). Languages: Spanish (native), English (fluent), Japanese (business level), French (intermediate).

Education
======
* **Doctor of Engineering (Ph.D. equivalent), Machine Learning**, Kanazawa University, Japan, 2009. Japanese Government (MEXT) scholarship.
  * Thesis: "Characterization of microarray dataset from the perspective of feature importance."
* **M.Sc., Engineering and Computer Science (Adaptive Systems)**, Kanazawa University, Japan, 2006. Also completed the Management of Technology (MOT) program.
  * Thesis: "Brain Computer Interface Based on ICA and Neural Network."
* **BE, Management of Information Systems**, Universidad Veracruzana, Mexico, 1998.
* Additional training: Singularity University Graduate Studies Program, inaugural class, NASA Ames Research Center (2009, Google full scholarship); MBA coursework, University of Louisville (2012); Business Administration, ITESM (2001); Robotics training, Kanazawa Institute of Technology, JICA program (2002).

Research and professional experience
======
* **Staff Scientist, Neural Computation Unit (Prof. Kenji Doya), OIST**, Okinawa, Japan. 2024 – present
  * Lead development of eNDM, a physics-based generative model of neurodegenerative-disease propagation on individual brain networks.
  * Designed an autonomous five-agent LLM research pipeline (Literature, Hypothesis, Experiment, Evaluation, Synthesis) run on an HPC/Slurm cluster; the same architecture supports the Triad Justice System, a judicial-process transparency platform developed with the founder of Kleros.
  * Participant in the Brain/MINDS 2.0 consortium.
* **Representative Director (founder, CTO), SURAIERA Inc. (formerly Skyspec KK)**, Japan. 2023 – present
  * Drone and AI company for wildfire and infrastructure monitoring; XPRIZE Wildfire semifinalist (2025); 2 patent applications.
  * Host organization in Okinawa for four Robotics and Computer Systems Engineering students from Tecnológico de Monterrey in the AD2026 learning program (September 2026 – January 2027), in coordination with Prof. Arturo Cerón.
* **Director, Exponential Japan KK**, Tokyo, Japan. 2017 – present
  * AI and digital-transformation programs for Sony headquarters and its global divisions, and other Japanese companies.
* **Emerging Technologies Senior Advisor, United Nations OICT** (remote). 2022 – 2023
* **Chair AI Researcher, FAIRE, The University of Tokyo**, Japan. 2017 – 2022
  * Deep-learning pipelines for neuromorphic sensors, a deep-learning API for Scratch, hyperspectral-imaging machine learning for Fujitsu.
* **Staff Scientist, OIST**, Okinawa, Japan. 2015 – 2017
* **Postdoctoral Associate, University of Louisville**, USA. 2010 – 2013
* **Analyst and Software Engineer, Veratech S.A. de C.V.**, Mexico. 2000 – 2001

AI for the space industry as Adjunct Senior Industry Fellow, University of South Australia (2023 – 2024). Support for Japanese startups with the SingularityU Tokyo Chapter and Global Innovation Community.
